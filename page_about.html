About Us

About HLURU (Hluru China)

Founded in 2010 in Shenzhen, China, HLURU designs, tunes and produces hand-made healing instruments in our own factory. What started as a workshop for steel tongue drums grew into a full product line covering handpans, kalimbas (thumb pianos), string harps, cajons and wind chimes. Today our instruments are shipped to musicians, retailers and distributors in more than 60 countries across Europe, North America, Japan, South Korea, Australia, the Middle East and South America.

Every instrument passes through the same path: CNC cutting, hand-tuning, surface finishing, hand-percussion sampling, and final QC by an experienced luthier. We accept OEM/ODM projects and offer private-label packaging for wholesale buyers.

Our Mission

To make hand-tuned, concert-quality healing instruments affordable for the global music-therapy and sound-bath communities, while keeping every step of production under one roof — from raw steel to finished instrument.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Direct-from-factory pricing. No middlemen, no export agents — you talk to the workshop that tunes your instrument.
  • Hand-tuning by experienced luthiers. Every tongue, key and drum membrane is checked against a reference tuning fork.
  • OEM/ODM ready. Custom scale layouts, custom finishes, custom logos, custom carry bags. MOQ as low as 20 units per model.
  • Wholesale programmes. Tiered pricing, branded packaging dropship and 12-month replacement warranty.
  • CE / RoHS documentation. Export paperwork, MSDS sheets and carton specifications supplied with every order.
  • After-sales in English. Direct WhatsApp and email support, average response within 12 hours.

Our Workshop

Our 4,200-square-metre workshop in Shenzhen houses CNC cutting, laser etching, hand-tuning rooms, an anechoic test chamber, plus a small recording booth for sound-bath demonstrations. We host buyer visits year-round — see the factory-show page for the latest photos.
