Handpan vs Steel Tongue Drum: Which Should You Buy?

Both instruments are made from tuned steel, but they sound and play very differently.

Handpan

Two shells tuned against each other, ringed notes, played with the fingers from above. Sound: ethereal, organ-like, complex sustain. Price: $250-700. Weight: 4-7 kg. Learning curve: weeks to months.

Steel tongue drum

One shell, slot tongues pointing up, played with mallets or fingers. Sound: warm, marimba-like, calm sustain. Price: $60-200. Weight: 2-3 kg. Learning curve: hours.

Which to pick?

  • Pick a handpan if: you want the "Wow" sound for performance / recording, you don't mind the practice time, budget is $300+.
  • Pick a steel tongue drum if: you want a low-effort meditation instrument, you might give it as a gift, budget is under $150.

Pair them

Lots of our customers end up with one of each. They complement each other beautifully in sound-bath sessions — start the session with the deep handpan drone and transition to a tongue-drum melody in the middle.
