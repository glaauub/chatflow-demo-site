11 Notes vs 15 Notes Tongue Drum: Which Is Right For You?

Both 11-note and 15-note steel tongue drums are great picks — the right one depends on what you want to play and where you'll play it.

11-note tongue drum

The 11-note is a great beginner / mid-level instrument. Most are tuned in D Akebono or D Major and stay in one key, so you cannot play a wrong note. Each tongue is wider, easier to tap, and the resonance is more meditative than melodic. This is the drum we recommend for:

  • Sound-bath sessions and meditation
  • Yoga classes and mindfulness circles
  • People with no musical background

15-note tongue drum

The 15-note adds the chromatic notes that let you play songs. It is still mostly in one key (D Major) but the extras give you a wider range — useful if you want to play melodies, not just chord pulses.

Which should you order?

For therapy, calming and beginner playing: 11-note. For playing actual songs: 15-note. Many of our wholesale customers list both, since they appeal to slightly different buyers.
