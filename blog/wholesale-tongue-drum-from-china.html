Wholesale Tongue Drums From China: A Buyer's Guide To OEM

Sourcing healing instruments from China can save you 40-60% on retail pricing if you pick the right supplier. Here's a practical playbook.

Minimum order quantities

Most factories — including HLURU — accept 20-unit trial orders. Below that, expect to pay 30-50% more per unit and lose the OEM customisation options.

Lead time

  • Stock finish (silver/black/brown/gold) — 7-10 working days
  • Custom colour — 25 working days
  • Custom scale layout — 25-35 working days (includes a final sample approval)

Incoterms

EXW Shenzhen (you arrange everything) is the cheapest. FOB Shenzhen, CIF (your port) and DDP (your door) are also available — DDP is the easiest if you are a smaller shop.

Certifications

Ask for CE, RoHS, REACH, and — for EU buyers — a Prop 65 statement if you sell in California. Reputable factories provide these documents upfront.

5 red flags to avoid

  1. Factory asks for 100% deposit before production starts
  2. No sample offered before bulk, or sample cost is 2x the eventual bulk price
  3. Quoted prices 30%+ below market (suggests hidden fees or substandard materials)
  4. No workshop / testing room photos
  5. No clear MOQ or rejection policy in writing

If a supplier clears those filters, order 1 mixed sample to test the tuning, packaging and communication before placing a 50-unit bulk order.
