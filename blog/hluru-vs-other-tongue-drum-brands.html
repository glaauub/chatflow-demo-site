HLURU vs Other Tongue Drum Brands: A Buyer's Comparison (2025)

The tongue-drum category exploded after 2018 — there are now 30+ Chinese factories making them. Picking a supplier is hard. Here is how we compare against four other well-known names.

Material: 304 stainless steel

We use 304 stainless for our Premium & Travel series. It is heavier than the carbon-steel competitors, but it is also more rust-resistant and holds a tuning longer. The triangle series uses a thicker 2-mm gauge for a deeper, darker resonance.

Tuning stability

Every drum is hand-tuned by an experienced luthier against a reference tuning fork before shipping. Our defect rate on tuning over a 6-month period is under 1.5%. Two of the four competitor brands we benchmarked against were sitting around 4-6%.

OEM / ODM

All our drums come with custom engraving, custom scale layouts, custom carry-bag printing, and gift-box packaging from as low as 20 units per SKU. Lead time is 7-10 days for stock finishes, 25-35 days for custom.

Bottom line

If you want a hand-tuned, fully OEM-able tongue drum with reliable lead times and low MOQs, HLURU is a strong pick. Order 1 sample drum first to judge the tuning and finish before placing a bulk PO.
