How To Tune A 21-Key Kalimba In 5 Minutes (Beginner Guide)

A 21-key kalimba in C Major is the most popular instrument in our kalimba line. Here's how to tune it.

Tools

  • The tuning hammer that came with your kalimba
  • A reference tuner app (e.g. insTuner on iOS / Android)
  • A quiet room

The 5-minute routine

Start at the centre and work outwards. Tap each tine, read the tuner, and adjust the spring-loaded bolt at the bottom of the tine:

  1. Pluck tine 1 — should be C4
  2. Move up the tines 1-by-1 to C, D, E, F, G, A, B, C5, D5, E5, F5, G5, A5, B5, C6, D6, E6, F6, G6
  3. For each tine, raise the pitch by turning the bolt clockwise, lower it counter-clockwise. Tiny adjustments — an eighth of a turn goes a long way.
  4. After every 5 tines, give the whole instrument a light shake and let it rest 30 seconds. Steel bends relax, so re-check the earlier tines once you have done the whole scale.

Common mistakes

  • Over-tightening — turn gently, a quarter turn maximum at a time
  • Tuning in a humid room — wood expands and your reading will be off
  • Skipping the rest step — kalimba tines shift slightly when neighbours are tuned
