Frequently Asked Questions

We are the direct manufacturer — HLURU runs its own 4,200 m² workshop in Shenzhen, China. Every instrument is hand-tuned in-house before packing, and CE / RoHS / REACH documents are issued under our own name.
Sample orders start from 1 unit. Wholesale MOQ is typically 20 units per SKU for OEM / ODM, with mixed-SKU containers accepted for first-time buyers. Standard catalog items have no MOQ.
Yes. We support full OEM / ODM: custom scale layouts, finishes, laser engraving, private-label packaging and barcodes. Sample lead time is 7 days; bulk orders 30–45 days depending on quantity.
Popular scales are D Akebono, D Kurd, D Major, C Akebono, C Major, and C Minor. We also tune to 432 Hz or custom frequencies on request. Each drum leaves the workshop with a tuning certificate.
Samples by DHL / FedEx / UPS (3–7 days). Bulk orders by sea (FCL 20ft / 40ft) to all major ports. We handle FOB Shenzhen, CIF and DDP shipping, and provide commercial invoices, packing lists and certificates for customs clearance.
12-month replacement warranty against manufacturing defects. We keep a buffer stock of replacement tongues and shells, so any warranty claim is typically resolved within 7 days of receiving the photos.
Yes. Visits are welcome by appointment — send us your travel dates and we will arrange a factory tour plus a tuning demonstration. We are located in Bao'an District, 40 minutes from Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport.
Send an email to pupastrhynerogn@hotmail.com with your company name, target markets and the SKUs you are interested in. We reply within 24 hours with a tiered price list, sample options and lead times.
